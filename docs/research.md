## 2. Key Players Analysis

### 2.1 Glovo (Market Leader)
- **Founded:** 2014 (global), entered Nigeria 2021
- **Generated Revenue for Partners:** N71 billion since Nigeria launch
- **Partner Network:** 6,000+ Nigerian businesses (restaurants, grocery outlets, SMEs)
- **Presence:** Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt
- **Strengths:**
  - Strong brand recognition
  - Multi-category platform (food, groceries, pharmacy)
  - Established logistics infrastructure
  - Significant venture backing
- **Weaknesses:**
  - Higher commission rates (15-30%)
  - Limited focus on local Nigerian restaurants vs. chains
  - Premium pricing positioning

### 2.2 Chowdeck (Fast-Growing Challenger)
- **Founded:** 2021, Lagos-based
- **Funding:** $9M Series A (2025) from Noavstar, Y Combinator
- **Monthly Orders:** 1 million+ orders (October 2025 milestone)
- **Delivery Time:** Average 30 minutes
- **Expansion:** Launching quick commerce stores (40 by end of 2025, 500 by 2026)
- **Recent Move:** Acquired Mira (POS solution provider) for restaurant tech integration
- **Strengths:**
  - Proven profitability
  - Fast delivery (30-min average)
  - Strong local restaurant network
  - Data-driven approach
  - Aggressive expansion
- **Weaknesses:**
  - Still building national presence
  - Focus on major cities primarily

### 2.3 HeyFood
- **Founded:** 2021
- **Coverage:** 8 Nigerian cities (Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Ibadan prominent)
- **Strengths:**
  - Broad geographic coverage
  - Diverse restaurant selection (African staples, international cuisines)
  - Strong rider network
- **Weaknesses:**
  - Smaller scale vs. Glovo/Chowdeck
  - Less tech innovation visible

### 2.4 FoodCo
- **Type:** Supermarket chain with delivery service
- **Presence:** Lagos, Southwest Nigeria
- **Strengths:**
  - 30+ years brand trust
  - Physical store infrastructure
  - 23 stores across region
- **Weaknesses:**
  - Limited to grocery/food
  - Not a pure-play delivery platform

### 2.5 ChowHub
- **Founded:** 2015, Abuja-based
- **Focus:** Online food exploration and delivery
- **Strengths:**
  - Early mover in Abuja
  - Local market knowledge
- **Weaknesses:**
  - Limited scale
  - Smaller funding base

### 2.6 FoodLocker
- **Focus:** B2B food distribution + consumer delivery
- **Strengths:**
  - Unique farm-to-restaurant supply chain
  - Pickup and delivery options
- **Weaknesses:**
  - B2B primary focus
  - Less consumer-facing brand

### 2.7 Jumia Food (Exiting)
- **Status:** Shutting down food delivery in Nigeria and 6 other African countries (end of 2025)
- **Previous Position:** Once market leader
- **Reason for Exit:** Unprofitable operations, high operational costs

## 4. Commission Rates & Fee Models

### Industry Standard (Global Context)
- **Commission Range:** 15-35% per order
- **Typical Nigerian Market:** 15-25% for standard service

### Competitor Fee Structures

| Platform | Commission Rate | Delivery Fee Model |
|----------|---------------|-------------------|
| Glovo | 15-30% | Variable by distance |
| Chowdeck | ~15-20% | Flat rate + distance |
| HeyFood | 15-25% | Zone-based |
| Industry Avg | 15-25% | Distance-based |

### Restaurant Pain Points
- High commission eating into margins (15-30%)
- Payment delays
- Minimum order requirements
- Promotional fees

### Delivery Fee Structure
- **Base Fee:** N200-500
- **Distance Fee:** N50-150 per km
- **Peak Hours:** 1.2-1.5x multiplier
- **Average Order Value:** N3,000-8,000

## 5. Gaps & Opportunities

### Market Gaps

1. **Secondary Cities Underserved**
   - Port Harcourt, Ibadan, Kano have limited options
   - Opportunity: Geographic expansion

2. **Local Restaurant Integration**
   - Major platforms focus on chains
   - Opportunity: Partner with local "mama" restaurants

3. **Quick Commerce**
   - Chowdeck expanding to 500 quick stores
   - Opportunity: Ultra-fast delivery for essentials

4. **Lower Commission Pressure**
   - Restaurants frustrated with 15-30% commissions
   - Opportunity: Offer 10-15% for first 6 months

5. **Tech Integration Gap**
   - Many restaurants lack POS/ordering systems
   - Opportunity: SaaS tools for restaurants

6. **Subscription Models**
   - No dominant subscription service
   - Opportunity: Monthly fee for free delivery

### Opportunities Matrix

| Opportunity | Market Gap | Competitive Advantage Potential |
|-------------|-----------|-------------------------------|
| Lower commissions | Restaurant pain point | High differentiator |
| Secondary city focus | Underserved markets | First-mover advantage |
| Local restaurant network | Chain-heavy platforms | Authentic offering |
| Quick commerce | Emerging trend | Scalable model |
| Restaurant SaaS | Tech gap | Ecosystem lock-in |

- Tech/Development: N3-5M
- Marketing: N5-8M
- Operations: N3-5M

**Variable Costs (Per Order):**
