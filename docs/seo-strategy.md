## 1. Executive Summary

QuickBite enters a growing but competitive Nigerian food delivery market (valued at $8.53B+ in 2025, projected to exceed $16B by 2030). The primary competitors are **Jumia Food (exiting end-2025)**, **Glovo**, and **Chowdeck** (primary threat: 30-min delivery benchmark, Mira POS acquisition). This strategy prioritizes high-impact, low-cost organic channels first, then scales paid channels once traction is established.

## 3. On-Page SEO Recommendations

### 3.1 Current Landing Page Issues & Fixes
**URL:** https://dulcet-panda-80d15a.netlify.app

The current landing page should be audited and updated with the following on-page SEO elements:

#### Title Tags
- **Homepage:** `QuickBite — Food Delivery in Lagos | Order Nigerian Food Online` (≤60 chars)
- Append city-specific modifiers: `QuickBite — Food Delivery Lagos | Order Jollof, Amala & More`

#### Meta Descriptions
- **Homepage:** `QuickBite delivers Nigeria's favorite meals — jollof rice, suya, swallow & more — to your door in Lagos. Fast, affordable food delivery. Order now!` (≤155 chars)
- **Restaurant pages:** Dynamic meta descriptions with restaurant name + location + cuisine

#### Header Hierarchy (H1–H3)
```
H1: "Nigeria's Fastest Food Delivery in Lagos"
H2: "Order Local & International Cuisine"
H3: "Popular in Lagos"
H3: "How It Works"
H3: "Download the App"
```

#### Structured Data (Schema Markup)
- **Restaurant schema:** Name, address, rating, price range, cuisine type, delivery zone
- **FoodEstablishment schema:** Same as Restaurant
- **LocalBusiness schema:** Critical for local SEO (address, geo-coordinates, opening hours, phone)
- **FAQPage schema:** For the FAQ section (How long does delivery take? What are fees?)
- **BreadcrumbList schema:** Home > Lagos > [Restaurant Name]

#### Image Optimization
- All images: descriptive `alt` text (e.g., `alt="Jollof rice and grilled chicken delivered by QuickBite in Lagos"`)
- WebP format with lazy loading
- File naming: `jollof-rice-delivery-lagos.jpg` not `IMG_001.jpg`

#### Technical SEO Checklist
- [ ] Implement canonical tags (self-referencing on all pages)
- [ ] Submit XML sitemap to Google Search Console once domain is live
- [ ] Ensure `robots.txt` allows crawling of key pages
- [ ] Set up 301 redirects if any URL changes
- [ ] Enable HTTPS (Netlify provides this automatically)
- [ ] Add `hreflang` tags if expanding to other Nigerian cities

#### Content Recommendations for Landing Page
1. **Hero section:** Add a location-based CTA — "Enter your area/paste your address" with autocomplete
2. **Trust signals:** "500+ Lagos restaurants", "Average delivery 35 mins", "10,000+ orders delivered"
3. **Search box:** Prominent search bar for restaurant or cuisine search
4. **FAQ section:** 5–8 common questions (delivery time, fees, payment methods, cancellation)
5. **Footer:** Add business address (real or hub), phone number, email, social links

## 4. Local SEO Strategy

### 4.1 Google Business Profile (GBP) — Critical Priority

GBP is the single highest-ROI local SEO action for a Lagos-focused food delivery platform.

**Setup checklist:**
1. Create a GBP listing: Business type = "Food delivery service"
2. Business name: **QuickBite Delivery** (match exactly across all directories)
3. Address: Physical delivery hub address (even if residential/office — can be a shared hub in Lekki, VI, or Ikeja)
4. Service areas: Set to "Lagos" with specific neighborhoods: Lekki, Victoria Island, Ikoyi, Ikeja, Yaba, Surulere, Apapa
5. Phone: Nigerian mobile/landline number (+234-XXX-XXX-XXXX)
6. Website: Link to landing page
7. Hours: 7 AM – 11 PM daily (adjust as needed)
8. Categories: Primary = "Food delivery service"; Secondary = "Online marketplace"
9. Attributes: "Delivery", "Pickup", "Dine-in" (if applicable)

**GBP Optimization:**
- Upload 10–20 high-quality photos (food photos, delivery riders, branded packaging)
- Add your logo as the profile photo
- Fill out all business attributes (payment methods, dietary options, etc.)
- Write a compelling "From the business" description (750 characters max): "QuickBite is Lagos' newest food delivery platform connecting you to Nigeria's best local and international cuisine. Order jollof, suya, amala, burgers, pizza & more from 500+ restaurants across Lagos."
- Post weekly updates (offers, new restaurants, promos) using GBP posts

### 6.4 WhatsApp Strategy

WhatsApp is arguably the most important platform in Nigeria for commerce.

**Strategy:**

- **Broadcast Messages:** Weekly menu highlights, weekend promo alerts (opt-in, non-spammy)
- **Status Updates:** Daily (or every 2 days) WhatsApp Status posts — food images, promo banners, new restaurant features
- **Target:** 500 community members in 3 months; 2,000+ in 6 months

## 7. Paid Ads Strategy

**Budget Allocation Philosophy (Lean Startup Phase):**
- Prioritize organic channels first (SEO, social media, content)
- Paid ads as a scaling mechanism once product-market fit is confirmed
- Start with ₦50,000–₦100,000/month test budget; scale what works

### 7.1 Meta Ads (Facebook + Instagram)

**Campaign Objectives:**
1. **Awareness:** Video views / Reach (for brand building)
2. **Consideration:** Traffic / Engagement (drive to landing page)
3. **Conversion:** Lead generation / App installs (future app phase)

**Ad Formats:**
| Format | Use Case | Targeting |
|---|---|---|
| **Instagram Feed Ads** | Food photography posts, restaurant showcases | Interest: Food, Restaurants, Nigerian cuisine; Location: Lagos; Age: 18–45 |
| **Instagram Reels Ads** | Short food reveal/trend content | Broad interest targeting + lookalike audiences |
| **Facebook Carousel Ads** | Showcase 3–5 restaurant dishes in one ad | Lookalike audience from website visitors |
| **Lead Gen Ads** | Collect phone numbers / email for WhatsApp list | Location: Lagos; Interest: Food delivery, Online shopping |
| **Story Ads** | Promo/flash sale announcements | Retargeting warm audience |

**Targeting Parameters (Initial Test):**
- Location: Lagos (precise: Lekki, VI, Ikoyi, Ikeja, Yaba — radius targeting)
- Age: 18–40
- Interests: Food, Restaurants, Nigerian cuisine, Online food ordering, Delivery services
- Behavioral: "Mobile device users" (important for app-first behavior)
- Budget: ₦3,000–₦5,000/day for first 30 days across all ad sets

**Creative Best Practices:**
- Use close-up food imagery (mouth-watering shots)
- Front-load text: "Order Jollof Rice 🍛 Delivered to Your Door in Lagos"
- Add social proof: "500+ restaurants on QuickBite"
- Clear CTA: "Order Now" / "Get Free Delivery"
- Video ads: 6–15 seconds, loop seamlessly
- Use WhatsApp as a conversion fallback (many Nigerian users click "Message on WhatsApp" instead of filling forms)

### 7.2 Google Ads

**Campaign Types:**
1. **Search Ads** (highest intent) — Bid on purchase-intent keywords
2. **Performance Max** — Automated ads across Google's inventory (YouTube, Gmail, Search, Maps)
3. **Local Inventory Ads** (future) — When restaurant partner network is large enough

**Search Ad Keyword Targets:**

| Keyword | Match Type | Est. CPC (₦) | Campaign |
|---|---|---|---|
| food delivery Lagos | Exact | ₦80–₦200 | Search |
| order food online Lagos | Exact | ₦60–₦150 | Search |

**Display / Performance Max:**
- Target: Lagos-based users who have visited food delivery or restaurant websites
- Create asset groups: food imagery, rider imagery, brand logo, app screenshots
- Goal: Website traffic + app installs (once app is live)

**Budget Recommendation (Month 1–3):**
- Search Ads: ₦30,000–₦50,000/month
- Performance Max: ₦20,000–₦30,000/month
- Total Google Ads: ₦50,000–₦80,000/month

### 7.3 TikTok Ads

- **In-Feed Ads:** Native-looking video ads that appear in For You page
- **Targeting:** Interest: Food, Nigerian content, cooking content; Behavior: Mobile user, Online shopper

## 8. Competitor SEO Analysis

### 8.1 Key Competitors

| Competitor | Strengths | Weaknesses / Opportunities |
|---|---|---|
| **Jumia Food** | Brand recognition, large user base, established logistics, strong SEO DA | Broad e-commerce focus dilutes food message; perceived as expensive |
| **Glovo** | International brand, multi-category, strong app UX | Less local Nigerian food focus; expensive for users |
| **Chowdeck** | Ride-hailing brand trust, existing user base, aggressive pricing (30-min delivery, Mira POS acquisition creating restaurant tech threat) | Narrower restaurant selection, less local food depth |
| **ChowCentral** | Strong restaurant brand partnerships, premium positioning | Delivery-focused but limited app SEO |
| **Eatsy** | Growing local player, food-focused | Smaller scale, less domain authority |

### 8.2 Competitor SEO Positioning

| Competitor | Est. Domain Authority | Primary Keywords Ranking For | QuickBite Differentiation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jumia Food (exiting) | 75+ | "food delivery Nigeria", "order food online Lagos" | More focused, food-only, faster, cheaper perception |
| Glovo | 60+ | "food delivery Lagos", "grocery delivery Nigeria" | Hyper-focus on Lagos, better local food selection |
| Chowdeck | 50+ | "food delivery Lagos", "order food online Nigeria", "30 min delivery" | Local Nigerian food expertise, lower commissions, rider community |

### 8.3 Competitive Keyword Gaps (Opportunities for QuickBite)

These keywords have meaningful search volume but are not dominated by competitors:
- **"Nigerian food delivery Lagos"** — no strong competitor page exists specifically targeting this exact phrase
- **"order party jollof online Lagos"** — very low competition, high intent
- **"Nigerian swallow delivery"** — underserved by current players
- **"best food delivery for office Lekki"** — long-tail, office worker segment
- **"order food for office party Lagos"** — B2B food delivery angle
- **"food delivery app for Nigerian food"** — app-specific keyword

### 8.4 Competitor Content Analysis

| Competitor | Blog Active? | Content Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Jumia Food | Yes (Jumia blog) | General e-commerce, not food-focused |
| Glovo | Minimal | City guides, not deep food content |
